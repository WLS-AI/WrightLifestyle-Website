## Pricing research note
Draft pricing was checked against current London/UK hire examples on 15 September 2026. Examples included London bouncy castles around £90–£140+ and premium/luxury castles around £150–£180; soft play around £120–£295; and fun-food machine hire commonly around £150+ for standard machine hire, with staffed premium packages much higher. WrightLifestyle is positioned as a competitive starting-price offer rather than a bargain/low-quality offer.
